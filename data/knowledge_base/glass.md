# Glass Recycling

Glass is 100% recyclable and can be recycled endlessly without loss in quality or purity.

## How to prepare it
- Rinse bottles and jars to remove food/liquid residue.
- Remove lids and caps (these are often a different material and recycled separately).
- Labels can usually be left on — they burn off during the melting process.
- Keep broken glass separate and clearly labeled, or dispose of it in general waste,
  since broken shards are a safety hazard for waste handlers and can contaminate
  a batch of recyclable glass.

## What happens to it
Recycled glass ("cullet") is crushed, melted, and reformed into new glass containers.
Because glass doesn't degrade when recycled, this cycle can repeat indefinitely.

## Common mistakes
- Mixing broken window glass, mirrors, or drinking glasses with container glass —
  these have different melting points and chemical compositions and can ruin a
  recycling batch.
- Not rinsing containers, which can contaminate other recyclables (especially paper).
