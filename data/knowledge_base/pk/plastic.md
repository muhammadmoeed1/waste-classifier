# Plastic Recycling in Pakistan

Plastic has a real but more limited resale market than metal or paper in the local
informal economy. Clean PET bottles are the most reliably resalable plastic item;
many other plastic types have little to no local buyer.

## What a kabaria will take
- Clean PET bottles (drink bottles, resin code #1) are the most commonly bought
  plastic item — bottle collectors and kabarias both buy these, sometimes in bulk
  from shops, restaurants, or households.
- Some HDPE containers (resin code #2, e.g. certain bottles/jugs) may also be
  accepted, though less consistently than PET.

## Approximate resale rate
Illustrative ballpark: roughly **15-35 PKR per kg** for clean PET bottles — dirty,
mixed, or low-grade plastic is often worth much less or may not be accepted at all.
As with the other estimates here, treat this purely as a rough sense of scale.

## How to prepare it for resale
- Rinse bottles and remove caps/labels if practical — cleaner plastic sells for a
  better rate.
- Flattening/crushing bottles saves storage space for both you and the buyer.
- Keep different plastic types separate if you can; mixed loads are often paid at
  the rate of the lowest-value plastic in the bunch.

## What has no local resale path
- Plastic bags, film, wrap, multi-layer packaging (e.g. chip bags), and most
  polystyrene/styrofoam typically have no local buyer and go to general waste.
- Heavily soiled plastic (e.g. a takeout container full of sauce) is usually not
  worth a kabaria's time — rinsing first matters more here than for metal.
