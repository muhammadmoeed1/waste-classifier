# General Waste / Non-Recyclable Trash in Pakistan

Items in this category have no local resale path through the kabaria/scrap-dealer
economy and no municipal recycling stream either — they should go into general
household waste for collection (municipal or private garbage collection, depending
on the area).

## Examples of items with no local recovery path
- Styrofoam / expanded polystyrene.
- Soiled hygiene products (diapers, wipes) and food-contaminated packaging.
- Multi-material composite packaging (chip bags, some snack/coffee pouches).
- Thermal receipt paper, heavily laminated paper, and most broken glass.
- Mixed or heavily degraded plastics with no clean resin to sort.

## Special cases that are neither trash nor kabaria-resalable
- Electronics, batteries, and light bulbs contain hazardous materials and ideally
  should not go into general household waste at all — where possible, look for a
  dedicated e-waste collector or drop-off point (increasingly available in larger
  cities, though far from universal).
- Some informal scrap collectors specifically deal in electronics/e-waste for the
  metals inside (copper wiring, circuit boards) even though a general kabaria won't
  take an old phone or appliance the way they'd take a soda can.

## Why sorting still matters here
Even without kerbside recycling, separating out what a kabaria *will* buy (metal,
cardboard, paper, clean PET plastic) before putting the rest out as general waste
both earns a small amount and keeps genuinely resalable material out of a landfill
or open dump — which is where unsorted household waste in much of Pakistan
ultimately ends up.
