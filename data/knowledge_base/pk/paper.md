# Paper Recycling in Pakistan

Like cardboard, paper is part of the informal "raddi" (waste paper) trade rather
than any municipal recycling program. A kabaria, raddi shop, or even many
grocery/general stores will buy clean paper by weight.

## What a kabaria will take
- Newspapers, magazines, office paper, notebooks, and books are all commonly
  accepted as "raddi".
- Newspaper specifically is often the most sought-after paper type and can command
  a slightly better rate than mixed office paper.

## Approximate resale rate
Illustrative ballpark: roughly **10-20 PKR per kg** for clean, dry paper — newspaper
tends to sit at the higher end of that range. As with all figures here, this is a
rough sense of scale, not a fixed price; it depends on the dealer, the city, and
current paper-mill demand.

## How to prepare it for resale
- Keep it dry and free of food stains — wet or soiled paper is usually refused.
- Remove plastic covers, spiral bindings, and laminated pages where practical;
  these lower the resale grade.
- Shredded paper can usually still be sold but is often worth less per kg since it's
  harder for the buyer to bundle and resell.

## What has no local resale path
- Thermal receipt paper, heavily laminated paper, and tissue/paper towels are
  generally not wanted by raddi buyers and should go into general household waste.
