# Cardboard Recycling

Cardboard is one of the most widely recycled materials and is generally accepted
by curbside recycling programs almost everywhere.

## How to prepare it
- Flatten all boxes to save space and help sorting machinery at the recycling facility.
- Remove tape, staples, plastic windows (e.g. from packaging boxes), and any foam inserts.
- Keep it dry — wet or soaked cardboard loses fiber strength and is often rejected by recycling plants.
- Greasy cardboard (like a pizza box with oil stains) should NOT go in standard recycling;
  the greasy section should be torn off and placed in general waste or compost, while the
  clean part can still be recycled.

## What happens to it
Recycled cardboard is pulped, cleaned, and reprocessed into new cardboard, paperboard,
or other paper products. Cardboard can typically be recycled 5-7 times before its fibers
become too short to reuse.

## Common mistakes
- Leaving cardboard boxes un-flattened wastes truck space and can jam sorting equipment.
- Recycling wax-coated cardboard (e.g. some produce boxes) — this is usually NOT recyclable
  because the wax coating cannot be separated from the fibers.
